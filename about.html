ABOUT NEXT LEVEL

Our Story

History

Built for players. Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2023 in Johannesburg by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who wanted a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages can enjoy the latest gaming technology, compete in esports tournaments and socialise with friends.

Vision

Lead gaming culture.

Mission

Create great moments.

Goals

Fun, safe, inclusive.

Who We Serve

Individuals | Families | Schools | Gaming Clubs | Corporate Events

Why Choose Next Level?

We host gaming events, esports tournaments, birthday parties, school outings and corporate team-building events, with modern high-tech equipment and friendly staff.
