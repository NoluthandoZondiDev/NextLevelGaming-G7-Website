ABOUT NEXT LEVEL

Our Story

History

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2023 in Johannesburg by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who wanted a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages can enjoy the latest gaming technology, compete in esports tournaments and socialise with friends.

Jason noticed that serious gamers had few places to play on high-end equipment, while families and beginners had nowhere welcoming to try gaming for the first time. Next Level was built to fix both: a space where a first-timer and a tournament player feel equally at home.

Since opening, the arena has grown from a small gaming floor into a full venue offering premium gaming packages and individual experiences such as virtual reality, racing simulation and escape room challenges.

Our Journey

Vision

To be South Africa's leading gaming and esports destination, a place that leads gaming culture by bringing people together through technology, competition and fun.

We see a future where gaming is recognised as a skill, a sport and a social experience that everyone can take part in, whatever their age or background.

Mission

To create unforgettable gaming moments by providing:

  • Modern, high-performance gaming equipment and immersive technology
  • Friendly, knowledgeable staff who make every visitor feel welcome
  • Affordable packages and experiences for individuals, families, schools and businesses
  • A safe space to play, learn, compete and make friends

Goals

  • Fun: keep every experience exciting, fresh and worth coming back for
  • Safe: maintain a supervised, respectful environment for all ages
  • Inclusive: welcome beginners, casual players and competitive gamers alike
  • Competitive: host regular esports tournaments and training programmes
  • Growth: add new games and technology as the industry evolves
  • Community: build a local gaming community through events and partnerships

Our Values

  • Passion: we love gaming and it shows in everything we do
  • Respect: good sportsmanship on and off the screen
  • Teamwork: the best moments happen when people play together
  • Quality: well-maintained equipment and a clean, comfortable venue

Meet the Founder

Jason Naidoo is the founder and owner of Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena. A lifelong gamer, he turned his passion into a venue where the Johannesburg community can play, compete and connect.
